Before. During. After.

AfterBefore is not another feed built for staying home. Its value starts when you go out.

00:35

WHY IT IS DIFFERENT

We are not building another event list.

Presence graph

The difference between “I might go” and “I actually arrived” gives AfterBefore a signal standard event listings do not have.

Nightlife identity

Passport, reputation and contribution history create a reason to return even when the user is not buying a ticket.

Verified community data

Reviews and signals mean more when they come from people the system can reasonably confirm were actually there.

Venue intelligence

Venues can compare planned demand with real arrivals and activate quests instead of simply buying another banner ad.

PRIVACY & SAFETY

Nightlife is intimate. Its data should be treated that way.

Location = arrival confirmation

The product principle is to use location to confirm presence, not to sell an individual's movement trail.

Visibility stays under user control

Social features should remain opt-in, with block/report controls and minimal unnecessary exposure of personal data.
