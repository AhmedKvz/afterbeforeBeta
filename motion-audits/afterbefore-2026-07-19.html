AfterBefore — a nightlife OS whose motion has real character, undercut by snaps, silence and a pulse epidemic

Belgrade nightlife operating system: mobile-first PWA with a terminal aesthetic (IBM Plex Mono, deep blacks, genre color wheel), a signature orb radial nav, a bottom-sheet venue surface and a playful dare/passphrase game layer. Used at night, in clubs, often one-handed.

React 18 + Vite · inline-style OS components · hand-rolled CSS keyframes (no framer-motion in the OS) · audited: src/os/** + landing
3Critical 3Important 4Opportunities
OVERALL

This is not AI-slop motion — the orb's springy radial open and the sheet's ease-out-quint curve show real authorship. But the app currently animates the decorations and snaps the structure: screens, overlays and tabs all cut instantly while ten pulsing dots loop forever. Invert that — put motion on state changes, take it off idle indicators — and add one true celebration to check-in, and the motion will match the brand's ambition.

CriticalJheyA11y

The wheel spin ignores prefers-reduced-motion

WhatThe spin is a JS setInterval cycling card content every 90ms with a blur flash. The CSS reduced-motion guards can't reach it — a motion-sensitive user who opted out still gets ~2.5s of rapid content flashing. Same class of issue: the dare-wheel and šifra overlays are full-screen instant swaps driven by JS state.

Why it mattersAccessibility is the one non-negotiable in this audit. Rapid full-card flashing is exactly the vestibular-trigger category reduced-motion exists for — and the game layer is the app's most attention-seeking surface.

Recommended motionGate the spin in JS: matchMedia('(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)') → skip the interval, land instantly on the result with a single soft blur-out settle (demo). Full experience for everyone else, instant honest landing for those who asked.

Issues to address
CriticalJakub

Overlays have no exits — and half have no enters

WhatClosing anything — sheet, dare wheel, campaign, crew chat, dance mode — is an instant unmount. The dare wheel and campaign don't even animate in: a full-screen dark surface just appears over the app.

Why it mattersExits are how an interface says "that happened." Instant disappearance after a 400ms entrance breaks the physics the enter established — it reads as a crash, especially full-screen. Jakub's rule: exits exist, and they're subtler than enters.

Recommended motionShared overlay pattern: enter opacity 0→1 + scale .96→1 at 200ms; exit mirror at 150ms, subtler (scale to .985). For unmount-on-state, keep a 150ms closing class before removal (or a tiny useOverlayExit hook — one implementation, five surfaces).
